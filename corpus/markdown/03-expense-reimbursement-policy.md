# Expense Reimbursement Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-003  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Finance & Human Resources  

---

## 1. Overview

Acme Corp reimburses employees for reasonable, pre-approved business expenses incurred in the performance of their job duties. This policy covers travel, home office equipment, meals, software, and other business-related expenses. Personal expenses are never reimbursable.

---

## 2. General Principles

- All expenses must have a **valid business purpose**.
- Expenses above $75 require a **receipt** (digital or physical).
- Expenses must be submitted within **30 days** of being incurred.
- Employees must submit via the **Concur expense system**.
- Manager approval is required for all submissions.
- Finance audits all expense reports; fraudulent claims result in disciplinary action up to termination.

---

## 3. Travel Expenses

### 3.1 Airfare
- **Economy class** required for flights under 6 hours.
- **Premium economy or business class** is permitted for flights 6 hours or longer (red-eye or overnight) or when the employee has a documented medical condition requiring it.
- Book at least **14 days in advance** when possible to reduce cost.
- Upgrades purchased personally are not reimbursable.

### 3.2 Hotels
- Up to **$250/night** in standard U.S. cities; up to **$350/night** in high-cost cities (NYC, SF, Boston, DC, Seattle).
- Must stay within the corporate travel portal (TravelPerk) to qualify for reimbursement.
- Incidentals charged to the room (movies, minibar) are not reimbursable.

### 3.3 Ground Transportation
- **Rideshare (Uber/Lyft):** Reimbursable to/from airports and between business locations.
- **Rental car:** Requires pre-approval from manager if renting for more than 1 day. Use compact or midsize class unless unavailable.
- **Personal vehicle mileage:** Reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate (67 cents/mile as of 2025). Submit mileage via Concur with origin and destination documented.
- **Parking and tolls:** Reimbursable with receipts.

### 3.4 Meals and Per Diem
- **Breakfast:** Up to $20
- **Lunch:** Up to $30
- **Dinner:** Up to $60
- **Total daily meal limit:** $110
- Alcohol is **not reimbursable** unless part of a pre-approved client entertainment event.

---

## 4. Home Office Equipment

### 4.1 Initial Setup Stipend (Full-Time Remote Employees Only)
- Employees approved for a **fully remote** primary work arrangement are eligible for a one-time **$500 home office setup stipend**.
- Eligible items: monitor, keyboard, mouse, desk accessories, webcam, headset.
- **Not eligible:** Desks, chairs, general furniture, personal décor, office supplies (these are listed separately below).
- Requires manager approval and submission of receipts via Concur within 90 days of remote work arrangement approval.

### 4.2 Desk and Chair
- Acme Corp does **not** reimburse desks, chairs, or other furniture as standard expense.
- **Exception:** Employees with a documented ergonomic or medical need may submit a request to HR. If approved by HR and the employee's doctor (via a signed note), up to **$400** may be reimbursed for an ergonomic chair or standing desk.

### 4.3 Internet Subsidy
- Full-time remote employees are eligible for up to **$50/month** internet subsidy.
- Submit monthly via Concur using your ISP invoice.
- This is a direct subsidy, not a taxable benefit.

### 4.4 Company-Issued Equipment
- Laptops and primary monitors are **company-issued** and must be requested through IT (see Equipment Policy POL-HR-008).
- Do not purchase a laptop and submit it as a reimbursable expense — this will be denied.

---

## 5. Software and Subscriptions

- Software purchased for business use must be approved by IT before purchase.
- Individual software subscriptions (SaaS tools) are reimbursable up to **$50/month** per tool with manager approval.
- Subscriptions exceeding $50/month or $500/year require Finance pre-approval.
- Personal subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, personal cloud storage) are **not reimbursable**.

---

## 6. Client Entertainment

- Client meals and entertainment require pre-approval from the employee's manager.
- Must document: business purpose, names and companies of all attendees, and outcome.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only at client entertainment events with manager approval, up to **25% of the total bill**.
- Gifts to clients: up to **$50 per recipient per year** with manager approval. Cash gifts are never permitted.

---

## 7. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are explicitly **not** reimbursable:
- Personal meals when not traveling on business
- Commuting costs (home to primary office)
- Personal grooming, clothing, gym memberships
- Traffic fines, parking tickets
- Lost or stolen personal property
- Home utilities (electricity, heating, rent)
- Family member travel costs
- Political donations or contributions
- Any expense lacking a receipt over $75

---

## 8. Reimbursement Timeline

- Approved expenses are processed within **5–7 business days** of manager approval in Concur.
- Payments are made via direct deposit to the employee's bank account on file.
- Late submissions (over 30 days) require VP approval to process.
