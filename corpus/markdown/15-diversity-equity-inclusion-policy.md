# Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-015  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Human Resources & DEI Office  

---

## 1. Overview

Acme Corp believes that a diverse workforce and an inclusive culture make the company stronger and better able to serve its customers. This policy describes Acme Corp's commitments and programs related to diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI), and complements the Equal Employment Opportunity and anti-harassment commitments in the Workplace Conduct Policy (POL-HR-006).

---

## 2. Hiring Commitments

- Every open role's interview panel must include at least one member from a different team than the hiring manager, to reduce in-group hiring bias.
- Job postings are reviewed for inclusive language before posting, and salary ranges are included on every external posting in accordance with applicable state pay-transparency laws.
- Acme Corp partners with organizations supporting historically underrepresented groups in tech (including but not limited to AnitaB.org, /dev/color, and Lesbians Who Tech) for recruiting outreach, without using quotas in final hiring decisions, which remain based solely on qualifications and interview performance.

---

## 3. Employee Resource Groups (ERGs)

Acme Corp sponsors several voluntary Employee Resource Groups, each with an annual budget for events and an executive sponsor:

| ERG | Focus | Meets |
|---|---|---|
| Acme Women's Network | Women in the workplace, mentorship, career growth | Monthly |
| Acme Pride | LGBTQ+ employees and allies | Monthly |
| Acme Black Employee Network | Black and African American employees and allies | Monthly |
| Acme Parents & Caregivers | Working parents and caregivers | Bi-monthly |
| Acme Veterans Network | Veterans and military families | Quarterly |

Any employee may propose a new ERG by submitting a charter to the DEI Office; a new ERG needs at least 10 interested employees and an executive sponsor to be formally recognized and funded.

---

## 4. Accessibility and Accommodations

- Acme Corp provides reasonable accommodations for employees and candidates with disabilities, in compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and applicable state law.
- Accommodation requests are made through **accommodations@acmecorp.example.com** and are handled confidentially by HR in an interactive dialogue with the requesting employee; a request does not need to go through the employee's manager.
- All company offices meet ADA physical accessibility standards; digital tools and internal systems are evaluated annually against WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility guidelines.

---

## 5. Inclusive Benefits

- Acme Corp's health plans (POL-HR-004) cover gender-affirming care consistent with plan documents, and domestic partners are eligible for benefits enrollment on the same basis as spouses.
- Parental leave (POL-HR-010 §3) is available on an equal basis regardless of gender, marital status, or how a family welcomed a child (birth, adoption, or surrogacy).
- Religious accommodation requests (schedule adjustments for religious observance, dress code exceptions) are handled through the same accommodations process described in Section 4.

---

## 6. Training

- All employees complete an annual **Inclusive Workplace** training module, covering unconscious bias, inclusive communication, and Acme Corp's DEI commitments. This is separate from, and in addition to, the anti-harassment training required under the Workplace Conduct Policy (POL-HR-006).
- Managers and interview panel members complete additional **Inclusive Hiring** training before conducting interviews.

---

## 7. Measuring Progress

- The DEI Office publishes an annual, de-identified workforce demographics report internally, tracking representation at each level of the organization and pay-equity audit results (see the Compensation and Pay Policy, POL-HR-012 §6).
- DEI goals are reviewed by executive leadership quarterly alongside other company OKRs (see POL-HR-011 §3).

---

## 8. Reporting Concerns

Concerns about discriminatory treatment, exclusionary behavior, or a DEI program not functioning as intended can be raised through the same channels described in the Workplace Conduct Policy (POL-HR-006 §4), including anonymously via the ethics hotline. Retaliation for raising a good-faith DEI concern is strictly prohibited.
