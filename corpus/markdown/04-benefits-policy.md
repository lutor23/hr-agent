# Employee Benefits Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-004  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Human Resources – Benefits Team  

---

## 1. Overview

Acme Corp offers a comprehensive benefits package to support the health, financial security, and well-being of employees and their families. This document summarizes all benefit programs. Detailed plan documents are available on the Benefits Hub in Workday.

---

## 2. Eligibility

| Employee Type | Benefits Eligibility |
|---|---|
| Full-time (30+ hrs/week) | Full benefits package |
| Part-time (20–29 hrs/week) | Medical only (employee contribution increases); dental and vision optional |
| Part-time (<20 hrs/week) | Not eligible |
| Contractors / Interns | Not eligible |

Benefits begin on the **first day of the month following 30 days of employment** (e.g., hired Sep 15 → benefits effective Nov 1).

Dependents (spouse/domestic partner, children up to age 26) may be enrolled during the initial enrollment window or at qualifying life events (QLE).

## 3. Health Insurance

### 3.1 Medical Plans
Acme Corp offers three medical plan options through **BlueCross BlueShield**:

| Plan | Type | Employee Premium/mo | Company Contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acme Select | HMO | $85 (employee only) | 80% |
| Acme Flex | PPO | $145 (employee only) | 70% |
| Acme HDHP | HDHP + HSA | $45 (employee only) | 90% (inc. HSA contribution) |

### 3.2 Health Savings Account (HSA)
- Available only with the Acme HDHP plan.
- Acme contributes **$600/year** (individual) or **$1,200/year** (family) to the HSA.
- Employee may contribute additional pre-tax funds up to IRS limits ($4,150 individual / $8,300 family in 2025).

### 3.3 Dental
- Plan provider: **Delta Dental**
- Preventive care: 100% covered (no out-of-pocket)
- Basic restorative: 80% covered after deductible
- Major restorative: 50% covered after deductible
- Annual maximum benefit: $2,000 per person

### 3.4 Vision
- Plan provider: **VSP**
- Annual eye exam: fully covered
- Frames or contacts: up to $200 allowance per year

## 4. Retirement — 401(k)

- Provider: **Fidelity Investments**
- Employee contribution: up to IRS limit ($23,000 pre-tax in 2025; $30,500 if age 50+)
- **Company match:** 100% of the first 4% of salary contributed.
- Vesting schedule: Cliff vesting — 100% vested after **2 years** of service.
- Enrollment: Open at any time via Fidelity NetBenefits. Default enrollment at 3% after 90 days unless opted out.
- Investment options: 20+ funds including target-date funds, index funds, and a self-directed brokerage window.

---

## 5. Life and Disability Insurance

### 5.1 Basic Life Insurance
- Acme provides **2× annual base salary** in group life insurance at no cost to the employee.
- Maximum coverage: $500,000.

### 5.2 Supplemental Life Insurance
- Employees may purchase additional coverage (up to 5× salary) during open enrollment. Evidence of insurability required above $300,000.

### 5.3 Short-Term Disability (STD)
- Covers 60% of base salary for up to **12 weeks** after a 7-day waiting period.
- Funded by Acme; no employee premium.

### 5.4 Long-Term Disability (LTD)
- Covers 60% of base salary after STD ends, up to age 65 or recovery.
- Maximum benefit: $10,000/month.
- Funded by Acme; no employee premium.

## 6. Additional Benefits

### 6.1 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Free confidential counseling: up to **8 sessions per year** per issue (mental health, financial stress, legal advice).
- Provider: **Lyra Health**. Access 24/7 at 1-800-555-LYRA.

### 6.2 Commuter Benefits
- Pre-tax payroll deduction for transit passes and parking, up to IRS limits ($315/month transit, $315/month parking in 2025).

### 6.3 Professional Development
- Annual learning budget: **$1,500/year** for courses, certifications, and conferences.
- Submit request for approval via Workday before purchasing.

### 6.4 Tuition Reimbursement
- Up to **$5,250/year** (IRS tax-free limit) for job-related undergraduate or graduate coursework.
- Requires pre-approval from HR and manager.
- Employee must remain with Acme for 1 year after course completion or repay on a pro-rated basis.

### 6.5 Parental Leave
- See Parental Leave Policy (POL-HR-005) for full details.
- Summary: 12 weeks fully paid for primary caregiver; 4 weeks fully paid for secondary caregiver.

## 7. Open Enrollment

- Open enrollment occurs **annually in November** for the following plan year.
- Changes outside open enrollment are only permitted following a **Qualifying Life Event (QLE)**: marriage, divorce, birth/adoption, loss of other coverage, death of dependent.
- QLE changes must be submitted within **30 days** of the event.

---

## 8. COBRA Continuation

Upon separation from employment, employees may continue health coverage under **COBRA** for up to 18 months. COBRA premiums are paid entirely by the former employee (full cost + 2% administrative fee).

---

## 9. Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA)

- **Healthcare FSA:** Employees may elect to set aside up to **$3,300/year** (2025 IRS limit) in pre-tax dollars for eligible medical, dental, and vision expenses not covered by insurance. Available regardless of which medical plan the employee elects, including the HDHP (subject to IRS rules limiting HDHP enrollees to a Limited-Purpose FSA for dental/vision only).
- **Dependent Care FSA:** Employees may set aside up to **$5,000/year** (per household) in pre-tax dollars for eligible child care or elder care expenses, enabling the employee (and spouse, if married) to work.
- FSA elections are made during open enrollment (Section 7) and are generally locked in for the plan year except following a Qualifying Life Event.
- Healthcare FSA offers a **$660 carryover** into the following year; amounts above the carryover limit are forfeited under the IRS "use it or lose it" rule. The Dependent Care FSA has no carryover.
- FSA debit cards and reimbursement claims are administered through **WageWorks**; claims must include an itemized receipt.

## 10. Wellness Program

- Acme Corp's wellness program, administered through the **Virgin Pulse** platform, offers an annual **$300 wellness incentive** for employees who complete a biometric screening and accumulate a threshold number of wellness activity points (steps, workouts, mindfulness sessions) over the plan year.
- The incentive is paid as a contribution to the employee's HSA (if enrolled in the Acme HDHP) or as a payroll bonus (taxable) for employees on other plans.
- Gym membership discounts are available through the wellness platform's partner network at no direct cost to Acme Corp beyond the platform subscription.
- Biometric screening data is handled by the third-party wellness vendor under HIPAA protections; Acme Corp receives only aggregate, de-identified participation data, never individual health results.
- The wellness program is entirely voluntary; non-participation has no effect on an employee's health plan enrollment, premiums, or any other benefit described in this policy.
