# Equipment and Technology Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-008  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** IT & Human Resources  

---

## 1. Overview

Acme Corp provides employees with the tools and technology necessary to perform their job duties. This policy governs the procurement, use, care, and return of company-issued equipment.

---

## 2. Standard Equipment by Role

All full-time employees receive a **standard equipment package** at the start of employment, determined by role category:

### 2.1 Standard Package (All Full-Time Employees)
- **Laptop:** MacBook Pro 14" (engineers, designers) or MacBook Air 13" (all other roles)
- **Power adapter and cable**
- **Access to virtual desktop environment (Citrix) where applicable**

### 2.2 Engineering / Technical Roles (Additional)
- 27" external monitor
- USB-C hub / dock
- Mechanical keyboard and ergonomic mouse
- Noise-cancelling headset (Jabra or Bose)

### 2.3 Executive and Senior Leadership (Additional)
- 27" or 32" external monitor
- Premium headset
- iPad for travel (Director level and above, upon request and manager approval)

---

## 3. Requesting Equipment

### 3.1 New Hire Equipment
- Equipment is auto-provisioned based on role during the onboarding process.
- Remote employees: equipment ships to home address; expect delivery 2–3 business days before start date.
- On-site employees: equipment is available at the office IT desk on Day 1.

### 3.2 Equipment Upgrades and Replacements
- Submit requests via **Jira IT Service Desk** (project: ITHELP).
- Upgrades beyond the standard package require manager approval and Finance budget confirmation.
- Replacement due to normal wear: approved on a 3-year refresh cycle.
- Replacement due to damage: see Section 5.

### 3.3 Additional Peripherals
- Additional monitors, keyboards, mice, or other peripherals beyond standard: require manager approval.
- Budget limit for non-standard peripherals: **$200/year** without VP Finance approval.

---

## 4. Acceptable Use of Company Equipment

- Equipment is provided for **business use**; incidental personal use is acceptable provided it does not violate the Data Security Policy (POL-IT-001).
- Do not install unapproved software (use the Managed Software Center / company app catalog).
- Do not lend equipment to family members, roommates, or other individuals.
- Do not use company equipment for personal income-generating activities.
- Equipment may be audited or monitored in accordance with applicable law and the Data Security Policy.

---

## 5. Damage, Loss, and Theft

### 5.1 Accidental Damage
- Report accidental damage to IT Helpdesk within **24 hours**.
- Minor damage (cracked screen, damaged port): IT will assess and replace at no cost for the first incident; a $150 charge applies for subsequent incidents within 12 months.
- Significant damage (liquid damage, structural damage): may result in an employee contribution per the damage assessment.

### 5.2 Loss or Theft
- Report lost or stolen equipment to IT Security (**security@acmecorp.example.com**) and your manager **immediately**.
- IT will remotely wipe the device.
- Intentional loss, negligence, or failure to report promptly may result in disciplinary action and/or a charge equal to the depreciated value of the equipment.

---

## 6. Equipment Care

- Store laptops in a padded bag when traveling.
- Do not leave equipment unattended in vehicles or public areas.
- Keep liquids away from laptops and peripherals.
- Do not apply stickers, paint, or permanent markings to company equipment (including asset tags).
- Clean equipment using a dry or lightly damp microfiber cloth; do not use solvents.

---

## 7. Equipment Return

### 7.1 Upon Separation from Employment
- All company equipment must be returned within **5 business days** of separation (resignation, termination, or end of contract).
- Remote employees: IT will send a prepaid return shipping label to the employee's home address.
- Outstanding equipment not returned within 30 days will be deducted from final pay where permitted by applicable law.

### 7.2 Role Changes
- If a role change results in a different standard equipment package, existing equipment will be evaluated and exchanged as appropriate.

---

## 8. Software Licensing

- Do not install unlicensed or pirated software on any company device.
- All software must be procured through IT or the approved vendor catalog.
- Employees who identify a software need not covered by the current catalog should submit a software procurement request via Jira IT Service Desk.
