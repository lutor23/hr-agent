# Code of Business Conduct and Ethics Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-017  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Legal & Human Resources  

---

## 1. Overview

This policy sets expectations for ethical business conduct at Acme Corp, covering conflicts of interest, gifts and entertainment, anti-bribery and corruption, confidential and insider information, and how to raise an ethics concern. It is distinct from, and complements, the Workplace Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy (POL-HR-006), which covers interpersonal workplace behavior.

---

## 2. Conflicts of Interest

- A conflict of interest arises when an employee's personal interests (financial, familial, or otherwise) could improperly influence, or appear to influence, their business decisions on behalf of Acme Corp.
- Common examples requiring disclosure: a close family member working at or owning a vendor/competitor/customer; a significant personal financial stake in a company Acme Corp does business with; outside employment or board service that could compete with or interfere with your Acme Corp role.
- Employees must disclose potential conflicts to their manager and to **ethics@acmecorp.example.com** as soon as they are identified, using the Conflict of Interest Disclosure form. Most disclosed conflicts can be appropriately managed (e.g., recusal from a specific decision) rather than prohibited outright.
- Managers may not supervise, hire, or set compensation for a family member or someone with whom they have a close personal relationship without an approved management plan from HR.

---

## 3. Gifts and Entertainment

- Employees may accept or offer **modest, occasional** business gifts and entertainment (a meal, a reasonably priced holiday gift) consistent with normal business courtesy.
- Gifts or entertainment with a value over **$150**, or anything offered in connection with a specific pending business decision (an active RFP, contract negotiation), must be reported to **ethics@acmecorp.example.com** before being accepted, and may need to be declined or redirected to a charitable donation.
- Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (gift cards above nominal value) must never be accepted from or given to vendors, customers, or business partners, regardless of amount.
- Government officials are subject to stricter, often much lower, gift limits under applicable law; employees interacting with government officials on Acme Corp's behalf should consult Legal before offering anything of value.

---

## 4. Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption

- Acme Corp strictly prohibits bribery or corruption in any form, anywhere it does business, consistent with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and the UK Bribery Act where applicable.
- "Facilitation payments" (small payments to expedite routine government action) are prohibited even where locally customary, except in a genuine imminent threat to health or safety, which must be reported to Legal immediately afterward.
- Employees working with third-party agents, consultants, or distributors on Acme Corp's behalf are responsible for ensuring those parties are also held to these anti-bribery standards; Legal maintains a due-diligence process for engaging new international intermediaries.

---

## 5. Confidential Information and Insider Trading

- Employees must protect Acme Corp's confidential and proprietary information (product plans, financial results before public release, customer data) consistent with the Data Security and Acceptable Use Policy (POL-IT-001).
- Employees who become aware of material non-public information about Acme Corp, or about a company Acme Corp is considering acquiring or partnering with, may not trade in that company's securities or share the information with others ("tipping") until it becomes public. This applies to Acme Corp's own securities as well, for any employee granted equity.
- Acme Corp observes periodic "blackout periods" before quarterly earnings announcements during which employees with access to financial results may not trade Acme Corp securities; affected employees are notified directly by Legal.

---

## 6. Use of Company Resources and Outside Activities

- Company resources (funds, equipment, work time, Acme Corp's name or brand) should not be used for personal business ventures, political campaigns, or outside employment without specific approval.
- Reasonable, limited personal use of company equipment (per the Equipment and Technology Policy, POL-HR-008 §4) is permitted and is not considered a conflict of interest on its own.
- Employees considering outside board service, advisory roles, or a side business should disclose it per Section 2, particularly if it is in a related industry or could create a time-commitment concern.

---

## 7. Political Activity

- Acme Corp does not make corporate political contributions and does not endorse political candidates or parties as a company.
- Employees are free to engage in personal political activity on their own time, using their own resources, but may not suggest that any personal political view represents an official Acme Corp position, and may not solicit colleagues for political contributions during work hours.

---

## 8. Reporting Ethics Concerns

- Ethics concerns can be reported to a manager, to **ethics@acmecorp.example.com**, or anonymously through the third-party ethics hotline (details posted on the company intranet), the same hotline referenced in the Workplace Conduct Policy (POL-HR-006 §4).
- Acme Corp strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith ethics concern or participates in an ethics investigation, even if the concern is ultimately not substantiated.
- Every reported concern is reviewed by Legal and/or HR depending on its nature, with outcomes communicated to the reporter where legally and practically possible.

---

## 9. Contact

Ethics and compliance questions: **ethics@acmecorp.example.com**. Conflict of interest disclosures should use the Conflict of Interest Disclosure form linked on the company intranet.
