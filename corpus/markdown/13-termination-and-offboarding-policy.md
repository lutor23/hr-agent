# Termination and Offboarding Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-013  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Human Resources & Legal  

---

## 1. Overview

This policy describes how employment separations are handled at Acme Corp, whether voluntary (resignation, retirement) or involuntary (termination, layoff), and what both the employee and the company can expect during offboarding.

---

## 2. Types of Separation

- **Voluntary resignation:** The employee initiates the separation. See Section 3 for notice expectations.
- **Involuntary termination (for cause):** Initiated by the company due to performance, conduct, or policy violations. Typically follows a documented Performance Improvement Plan (POL-HR-011 §6) unless the cause is a serious conduct violation (see the Workplace Conduct Policy, POL-HR-006), in which case immediate termination without a prior PIP may be warranted.
- **Layoff (involuntary, not for cause):** A position elimination due to business needs, restructuring, or budget constraints, unrelated to the individual's performance. Layoff decisions are reviewed by HR and Legal for compliance with the WARN Act and applicable state equivalents when the scale requires it.
- **Retirement:** Treated as a voluntary resignation for administrative purposes, with some additional retirement-specific benefits continuation options coordinated through HR.

---

## 3. Notice Period

- Employees are asked, but not legally required, to provide **at least two weeks' written notice** of resignation to their manager and HR.
- Managers and above are encouraged to provide **four weeks' notice** where possible, given the additional transition planning typically required.
- Acme Corp reserves the right to accept a resignation effective immediately rather than working through the full notice period, at its discretion; in that case the employee is paid through what would have been their last working day under the original notice.
- PTO may not generally be used during a resignation notice period, per the PTO Policy (POL-HR-001 §6), except with HR and department-head approval.

---

## 4. Final Paycheck

- Final paycheck timing follows applicable state law, which varies by location: in many states this means the next regular payday, while some states (including California) require payment on the employee's last day for involuntary terminations.
- The final paycheck includes all earned wages, any accrued and unused PTO payout where required by state law (see POL-HR-001 §5 for the general carryover/forfeiture rule, which some states override with a payout requirement), and any outstanding expense reimbursements.
- Any outstanding company debts (e.g. a negative PTO balance from unearned time taken, or unreturned equipment charges per POL-HR-008 §5) may be deducted from the final paycheck only where permitted by applicable state law.

---

## 5. Benefits Continuation (COBRA)

- Medical, dental, and vision coverage end on the **last day of the month** in which employment ends.
- Departing employees receive a COBRA election notice within 14 days of their separation date, as required by federal law, describing how to continue health coverage at their own cost (see the Employee Benefits Policy, POL-HR-004 §8 for full COBRA terms).
- 401(k) balances remain the employee's property; departing employees can roll over, cash out (subject to tax implications), or leave the balance with the plan administrator depending on the balance size and plan rules.

---

## 6. Equipment Return and Access Revocation

- All company equipment (laptop, monitor, badge, phone if issued) must be returned per the Equipment and Technology Policy (POL-HR-008 §7), typically within 5 business days of the separation date, or shipped back using a prepaid label for remote employees.
- IT disables all system access (email, Slack, VPN, SSO) effective at end-of-day on the employee's last working day, except where an extended transition arrangement has been explicitly approved.
- Departing employees must return or securely delete any company data from personal devices, consistent with the Data Security and Acceptable Use Policy (POL-IT-001).

---

## 7. Exit Interview

- HR offers an exit interview to every departing employee, whether the separation was voluntary or involuntary. Participation is optional for voluntary departures.
- Exit interview feedback is aggregated and shared with leadership in de-identified form to inform retention and culture improvements; individual responses are not shared with the departing employee's direct manager unless the employee consents.

---

## 8. Rehire Eligibility

- Employees who leave in good standing (voluntary resignation with appropriate notice, or a no-fault layoff) are generally eligible for rehire and may reapply for future openings.
- Employees terminated for cause related to serious misconduct (as defined in POL-HR-006) are generally **not eligible for rehire**.
- Rehire eligibility status is recorded in the employee's HR file and reviewed by HR during any future application from that individual.

---

## 9. References

- Acme Corp's official policy is to confirm only **dates of employment and job title** when contacted for a reference, through the designated employment verification service, regardless of the reason for separation.
- Managers may provide a more detailed personal reference only with the departing employee's explicit written consent, and should avoid doing so in an official capacity.
