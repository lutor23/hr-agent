# Workplace Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-006  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Human Resources & Legal  

---

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corp is committed to maintaining a workplace that is respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and vendors in any workplace setting — including in-person offices, remote environments, and company-sponsored events.

---

## 2. Equal Employment Opportunity

Acme Corp is an equal opportunity employer. We prohibit discrimination in hiring, promotion, compensation, benefits, or any other term of employment based on:
- Race, color, national origin, or ethnicity
- Sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation
- Age (40+)
- Religion or creed
- Disability or medical condition
- Pregnancy or parental status
- Veteran or military status
- Any other characteristic protected under applicable federal, state, or local law

---

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

### 3.1 Harassment
Harassment includes any unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic that creates a hostile, intimidating, or offensive work environment, or that interferes with an employee's work performance. Examples include:
- Offensive jokes, slurs, or derogatory language
- Displaying offensive images or materials
- Unwanted physical contact
- Persistent unwanted communication after being asked to stop
- Mocking, mimicking, or singling out an individual based on a protected class

### 3.2 Sexual Harassment
Sexual harassment includes:
- **Quid pro quo:** Conditioning employment decisions on acceptance of sexual advances.
- **Hostile work environment:** Unwelcome sexual comments, jokes, gestures, images, or conduct that creates an offensive environment.
- Unsolicited personal compliments about appearance in a professional setting.
- Sharing sexually explicit material via any work channel.

### 3.3 Bullying and Intimidation
- Persistent belittling, humiliating, or undermining a colleague's work.
- Deliberate exclusion from work activities or communications without legitimate business reason.
- Threats or aggressive behavior, whether verbal, written, or physical.

### 3.4 Retaliation
Retaliation against any employee who reports harassment, participates in an investigation, or opposes discrimination is strictly prohibited and will itself be treated as a violation of this policy.

---

## 4. Reporting a Concern

Employees who experience or witness prohibited conduct have multiple reporting channels:

1. **Direct manager:** If you feel comfortable doing so and your manager is not involved in the conduct.
2. **HR Business Partner:** Contact **hr@acmecorp.example.com** or your assigned HR BP directly.
3. **Ethics Hotline (anonymous):** 1-800-555-ETHIC — available 24/7; anonymous reports accepted.
4. **Senior HR Leadership:** For concerns involving your HR BP, contact the VP of HR directly.

**All reports are taken seriously and investigated promptly.** Reports will be kept confidential to the extent consistent with conducting a thorough investigation and taking appropriate corrective action.

---

## 5. Investigation Process

1. HR opens an investigation within **2 business days** of receiving a complaint.
2. HR interviews the complainant, respondent, and relevant witnesses.
3. HR prepares a written findings report (shared internally; not shared with external parties).
4. Investigation is typically completed within **20 business days**. Complex cases may take longer.
5. Appropriate action is taken based on findings; both parties are notified of the outcome to the extent legally permissible.

---

## 6. Disciplinary Actions

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Verbal or written warning
- Mandatory training
- Transfer or reassignment
- Demotion
- Suspension
- Termination of employment (for serious or repeated violations)

Severity of discipline depends on the nature, frequency, and impact of the conduct, as determined through the investigation.

---

## 7. Manager Responsibilities

Managers have heightened obligations:
- Take all complaints seriously and report them to HR immediately — even if you are not the appropriate investigator.
- Never attempt to resolve harassment complaints informally without HR involvement.
- Do not discourage employees from using any reporting channel.
- Complete annual Anti-Harassment Management Training (mandatory for all people managers).

---

## 8. Workplace Violence

Threats of violence, physical assault, or destruction of property will result in immediate termination and may be referred to law enforcement. Any employee who receives a threat or witnesses threatening behavior must contact HR and their manager immediately.

Emergency: call 911. Internal security: **security@acmecorp.example.com** or ext. 5000.
