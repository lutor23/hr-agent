# Onboarding Policy and Checklist
**Document ID:** POL-HR-005  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Human Resources – Talent Acquisition  

---

## 1. Overview

A structured onboarding experience sets new employees up for success. This policy defines Acme Corp's onboarding program covering the period from offer acceptance through the end of the first 90 days.

---

## 2. Pre-Start (Before Day 1)

**HR Responsibilities:**
- Send offer letter and complete background check (typically 3–5 business days).
- Issue IT equipment request form and ship device at least 3 business days before start date.
- Send new hire paperwork packet via DocuSign (I-9, W-4, direct deposit, policy acknowledgments).
- Enroll new hire in Workday and grant appropriate system access based on role.
- Assign an onboarding buddy (peer-level colleague in the same or adjacent team).

**New Hire Responsibilities:**
- Complete all DocuSign documents within 48 hours of receiving them.
- Confirm start date and any accommodations needs with HR.
- Provide government-issued ID for I-9 verification (in-person or via authorized remote notary).

---

## 3. Day 1 Activities

- Welcome email from HR with first-week schedule.
- IT setup session: laptop configuration, Okta SSO, Slack, email, VPN.
- HR orientation (1 hour): benefits overview, handbook review, code of conduct acknowledgment.
- Meeting with direct manager: role expectations, team introduction, first project briefing.
- Security training module (mandatory, must be completed Day 1): covers acceptable use, password management, phishing awareness, and data handling.
- Office tour (or virtual office/tool walkthrough for remote hires).

---

## 4. First Week (Days 1–5)

- Complete all required compliance trainings in the LMS (Learning Management System):
  - Anti-harassment and anti-discrimination training (≤2 hrs)
  - Data privacy and GDPR awareness (30 min)
  - Insider trading and financial ethics (if applicable to role)
- Set up 1:1 cadence with manager (recommended: weekly for first 90 days).
- Meet with key stakeholders identified by manager.
- Review team OKRs and individual goal-setting for the quarter.
- Buddy check-in meeting (onboarding buddy assigned by HR).

---

## 5. First Month (Days 1–30)

**Learning Milestones:**
- Complete role-specific product/technical onboarding (delivered by team lead).
- Shadow at least 2 cross-functional meetings.
- Complete a small first deliverable (defined by manager during Day 1 meeting).

**Administrative:**
- Confirm benefits enrollment by Day 30 (deadline; changes not possible outside open enrollment after this).
- Set up 401(k) contribution in Fidelity NetBenefits.
- Confirm equipment is fully functional; raise any IT issues to helpdesk@acmecorp.example.com.

**Check-ins:**
- 30-day review meeting with manager: discuss initial impressions, clarify expectations, address gaps.

---

## 6. Days 31–90

- Work independently on assigned projects with regular manager check-ins.
- Complete any remaining compliance or role-specific training modules.
- Participate in at least one team retrospective or planning event.

**90-Day Review:**
- Formal 90-day check-in with manager and HR.
- Review initial performance against stated expectations.
- Identify any skills gaps and create a development plan.
- Confirm satisfactory completion of probationary period (Acme Corp does not have a formal probationary period, but the 90-day review shapes the first annual review cycle).

---

## 7. Remote Onboarding Considerations

Remote new hires follow the same program with the following modifications:
- IT equipment ships directly to home address; verify receipt 2 days before start.
- Day 1 HR orientation is conducted via video call (Zoom or Teams).
- Onboarding buddy must schedule at least 3 video check-ins in the first 30 days.
- Virtual "coffee chats" with 5+ team members encouraged in the first 2 weeks.
- All system access is provisioned remotely; IT helpdesk is available 8am–8pm ET for setup support.

---

## 8. Manager Onboarding Checklist

Managers must complete the following within the first week:
- [ ] Schedule daily 15-min check-ins for Week 1.
- [ ] Introduce new hire to all direct team members.
- [ ] Share team wiki/documentation access.
- [ ] Define and document 30/60/90-day expectations in writing.
- [ ] Assign onboarding buddy (HR can assist if needed).
- [ ] Add new hire to all relevant recurring meetings.
- [ ] Set up access to team tools (GitHub, Jira, Confluence, Looker, etc.) via IT ticket.

---

## 9. Onboarding Buddy Program

- Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy for the first 90 days.
- Buddy is a peer (not the new hire's manager) in the same or adjacent team.
- Buddy commitment: ≥1 hour/week for the first month, then as-needed.
- Buddy responsibilities: answer informal questions, make social introductions, share team culture norms, connect new hire to resources.
