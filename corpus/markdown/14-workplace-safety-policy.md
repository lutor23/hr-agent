# Workplace Safety and Emergency Procedures Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-014  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Human Resources & Facilities  

---

## 1. Overview

Acme Corp is committed to providing a safe working environment for all employees, whether working from an Acme office, a client site, or remotely. This policy covers general safety guidelines, office emergency procedures, workers' compensation, and injury reporting.

---

## 2. General Safety Guidelines

- All employees are expected to follow posted safety signage, keep walkways and emergency exits clear, and report any unsafe condition (frayed cables, blocked exits, broken equipment) to Facilities immediately via the internal ticketing system.
- Office kitchens and shared spaces must be kept clean to avoid pest and fire hazards; personal space heaters and candles are prohibited in all Acme offices due to fire risk.
- Visitors must be signed in at reception and escorted by an employee at all times while in non-public areas of any Acme office.

---

## 3. Office Emergency Procedures

### 3.1 Fire

- On hearing the fire alarm, employees must evacuate immediately via the nearest marked exit — do not use elevators. Each floor has a designated outdoor assembly point, posted near the elevator bank.
- Floor wardens (volunteer employees trained annually) sweep their assigned floor and confirm evacuation to the building's fire safety coordinator at the assembly point.
- Do not re-enter the building until the fire department or building management gives an official all-clear.

### 3.2 Severe Weather

- In the event of a tornado warning or similar severe weather alert, employees should move to the building's designated shelter area (interior stairwells, away from windows), as indicated on the posted evacuation map for each floor.
- Office closures due to severe weather (snowstorms, hurricanes) are communicated by **7:00 AM** local time via email and Slack; affected employees are not required to come in and are not penalized for the closure.

### 3.3 Medical Emergency

- Call 911 (or the local emergency number) immediately for any serious medical emergency. Do not wait for a manager's approval to call emergency services.
- Each Acme office has at least one AED (automated external defibrillator) and a stocked first-aid kit; their locations are posted at reception and on each floor.
- After emergency services are contacted, notify Facilities and HR as soon as reasonably possible so the incident can be properly logged.

### 3.4 Active Threat

- In an active threat situation, Acme Corp follows the nationally recognized **Run, Hide, Fight** guidance: evacuate if there is a safe path to do so, otherwise shelter in place and lock/barricade doors, and only as an absolute last resort take action to disrupt or incapacitate the threat.
- Call 911 as soon as it is safe to do so; do not assume someone else has already called.

---

## 4. Workers' Compensation

- Any employee injured in the course of performing their job — at an Acme office, a client site, or while working remotely during their normal working hours — is covered by Acme Corp's workers' compensation insurance, regardless of fault.
- Workers' compensation covers reasonable medical expenses related to the injury and a portion of lost wages during recovery, per the requirements of the applicable state's workers' compensation statute.
- Filing a workers' compensation claim does not affect PTO balance or standard employment status, and retaliation against an employee for filing a claim is strictly prohibited.

---

## 5. Reporting Workplace Injuries

- Any workplace injury, no matter how minor, must be reported to your manager and HR **within 24 hours**, using the online Incident Report form.
- Prompt reporting ensures timely medical care and protects the employee's ability to file a workers' compensation claim — delayed reporting can complicate or jeopardize a claim under some state laws.
- HR will follow up on every reported incident, and Facilities will investigate any incident related to a physical office hazard to prevent recurrence.

---

## 6. Remote Employee Safety

- Remote employees are expected to maintain a reasonably safe home workspace (clear walkways, proper ergonomic setup where feasible, functioning smoke detectors) but Acme Corp does not inspect home offices.
- An injury sustained while actively performing work duties during normal working hours in a remote employee's designated home workspace is generally covered by workers' compensation, subject to the same reporting requirements in Section 5; injuries during breaks, personal errands, or outside normal working hours are evaluated case by case under applicable state law.
- Acme Corp provides a one-time $150 home office ergonomic stipend for remote employees, separate from the home office equipment reimbursement described in the Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-HR-003 §4).
