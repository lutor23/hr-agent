# Performance Management and Reviews Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-011  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Human Resources  

---

## 1. Overview

Acme Corp's performance management program is designed to align individual contributions with company goals, provide employees with regular feedback, and create a fair, transparent basis for compensation, promotion, and development decisions. This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees who have completed their 90-day onboarding period.

---

## 2. Review Cycle

- **Annual review:** Conducted each year in November, covering the prior 12 months of performance. Results inform the following calendar year's merit increases (see the Compensation and Pay Policy, POL-HR-012).
- **Mid-year check-in:** A lighter-weight conversation in May/June focused on progress against goals and any course corrections needed. No formal rating is issued at the mid-year check-in.
- **New hire 90-day review:** All new employees receive an informal review from their manager at the end of their first 90 days, focused on onboarding progress and early expectations rather than a formal rating.
- Employees who join after **September 1** are generally excluded from that year's annual review cycle, given insufficient tenure to evaluate fairly, unless their manager requests an exception.

---

## 3. Goal Setting

- Acme Corp uses an **OKR (Objectives and Key Results)** framework. Employees set quarterly OKRs in collaboration with their manager, aligned to team and company priorities.
- OKRs should be entered into the performance system (Lattice) within the first two weeks of each quarter.
- Stretch goals are encouraged but should not be the sole basis for a rating — partial achievement of an ambitious goal can still reflect strong performance.

---

## 4. Performance Ratings

Acme Corp uses a 4-point rating scale for the annual review:

| Rating | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Exceptional | Consistently exceeds expectations; significant impact beyond role scope |
| 3 | Strong | Meets and frequently exceeds expectations |
| 2 | Meets Expectations | Reliably meets the core expectations of the role |
| 1 | Needs Improvement | Falls short of expectations in one or more key areas; triggers a Performance Improvement Plan (see Section 6) |

Ratings are calibrated across each department by the manager's leadership chain before being finalized, to reduce inconsistency between individual managers' rating tendencies.

---

## 5. Promotion Process

- Promotions are considered twice per year, aligned with the annual review cycle (effective January) and a smaller mid-year cycle (effective July) for time-sensitive cases.
- A promotion requires: a rating of **3 (Strong)** or higher in the most recent review, a completed promotion packet (manager-written justification plus two peer references), and approval from the manager's department head.
- Employees may self-nominate for promotion consideration by discussing it with their manager at least 6 weeks before the relevant cycle.
- Promotions are not retroactive; the new title and compensation take effect on the cycle's effective date, not the date of approval.

---

## 6. Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

- An employee who receives a rating of **1 (Needs Improvement)**, or who shows a sudden, significant decline in performance mid-cycle, is placed on a **Performance Improvement Plan**.
- A PIP is a formal, written document specifying: the specific performance gaps, measurable improvement goals, a timeline (typically **30, 60, or 90 days** depending on severity), and the support the manager and company will provide (training, mentoring, adjusted workload).
- HR must review and approve every PIP before it is issued to the employee, to ensure fairness and consistency.
- At the end of the PIP period, the employee either returns to standard performance management, or — if goals were not met — may face further action up to and including termination (see the Termination and Offboarding Policy, POL-HR-013).
- Being placed on a PIP does not by itself affect PTO eligibility, benefits, or other standard employment terms.

---

## 7. Manager Responsibilities

- Provide ongoing, informal feedback throughout the year — annual reviews should never contain surprises.
- Document significant performance conversations (positive and corrective) in the performance system as they happen, not retroactively at review time.
- Complete all direct reports' reviews by the published deadline each cycle; late reviews delay that employee's compensation changes.
- Never discuss one employee's rating or compensation with another employee.

---

## 8. Employee Rights

- Employees have the right to see their own written review and rating, and to add written comments to it.
- Employees who disagree with their rating may request a review by their manager's manager within **10 business days** of receiving the rating.
- Retaliation against an employee for raising a good-faith disagreement with a rating is strictly prohibited and should be reported per the Workplace Conduct Policy (POL-HR-006).
