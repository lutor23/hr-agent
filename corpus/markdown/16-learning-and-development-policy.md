# Learning and Development Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-016  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Human Resources  

---

## 1. Overview

Acme Corp invests in employee growth through a combination of a professional development stipend, tuition reimbursement for further education, an internal learning platform, and a conference/training budget. This policy describes eligibility and how to access each program.

---

## 2. Professional Development Stipend

- Every full-time employee receives an annual **$1,500 professional development stipend**, usable for online courses, books, certifications, professional association memberships, or industry conferences.
- Part-time employees (20–29 hours/week) receive a prorated stipend of **$900** per year.
- Unused stipend does not roll over to the following year and is not paid out as cash.
- Purchases are submitted through the standard expense process (see the Expense Reimbursement Policy, POL-HR-003) with "Professional Development" selected as the expense category.

---

## 3. Tuition Reimbursement

- Acme Corp reimburses up to **$5,250 per calendar year** (the current IRS tax-free education assistance limit) for tuition toward a degree or certificate program relevant to the employee's current role or a reasonable future role at Acme Corp.
- Eligibility requires at least 6 months of tenure and a grade of **B or better** (or "Pass" for pass/fail courses) for reimbursement to be issued; grades must be submitted to HR within 60 days of course completion.
- Employees who voluntarily resign within **12 months** of receiving tuition reimbursement are required to repay the reimbursed amount on a prorated basis, as described in the tuition reimbursement agreement signed at the time of the request.
- Pre-approval from the employee's manager and HR is required **before** enrolling in a course for it to be eligible for reimbursement; retroactive requests for already-completed coursework are not accepted.

---

## 4. Internal Learning Platform

- All employees have access to Acme Corp's internal learning platform (LinkedIn Learning), offering self-paced courses on technical skills, leadership, and professional effectiveness at no cost beyond the standard company license.
- Certain compliance-related courses (security awareness per POL-IT-001 §8, anti-harassment per POL-HR-006, Inclusive Workplace per POL-HR-015 §6) are mandatory and assigned automatically; completion is tracked by HR.

---

## 5. Conference and External Training Budget

- Beyond the individual professional development stipend, each department maintains a separate **team training budget**, allocated by the department head, for larger expenses like major industry conferences, specialized technical certifications, or team-wide training engagements.
- Requests for team training budget use go through the employee's manager, who prioritizes requests against the department's available budget for the year.
- Travel for approved conferences follows the standard Travel Expenses rules in the Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-HR-003 §3).

---

## 6. Mentorship

- Acme Corp's formal mentorship program pairs employees with a mentor outside their direct reporting line, focused on career development conversations rather than day-to-day work guidance.
- New hires are automatically paired with an onboarding buddy for their first 90 days under the separate Onboarding Buddy Program (see the Onboarding Policy, POL-HR-005 §9); the mentorship program is a longer-term, opt-in program open to all employees after their first 90 days.

---

## 7. Internal Mobility

- Employees in good standing (not on an active Performance Improvement Plan, see POL-HR-011 §6) are encouraged to apply for internal job openings after at least **12 months** in their current role, or sooner with their current manager's support.
- Internal candidates interview through the same process as external candidates for the role, but hiring managers are expected to give internal candidates' applications a response within 5 business days given the ongoing-employment context.
