# Compensation and Pay Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-012  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Human Resources & Finance  

---

## 1. Overview

This policy explains how and when Acme Corp employees are paid, how overtime and exempt status work, and how salary changes, bonuses, and pay equity are handled. It applies to all employees, contractors are paid under separate contractor agreements and are not covered here.

---

## 2. Pay Periods and Paydays

- Acme Corp pays on a **biweekly** schedule — every other Friday, 26 pay periods per year.
- If a scheduled payday falls on a bank holiday, pay is issued on the **preceding business day**.
- Pay is distributed by **direct deposit only**; Acme Corp does not issue physical paychecks except in extraordinary circumstances approved by Finance.
- New employees should expect their first paycheck on the second payday following their start date, due to standard payroll processing lead time.

---

## 3. Overtime and Exempt Status

- **Non-exempt employees** (primarily hourly roles) are paid **1.5x** their regular hourly rate for all hours worked beyond 40 in a single work week, in accordance with the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) and applicable state law.
- **Exempt employees** (most salaried roles meeting FLSA executive, administrative, or professional exemption criteria) are not eligible for overtime pay and are compensated via a fixed salary regardless of hours worked in a given week.
- Non-exempt employees must record all hours worked in the timekeeping system (Workday) and must obtain manager approval **before** working overtime hours whenever possible.
- Misclassification concerns (believing your role is incorrectly classified as exempt or non-exempt) should be raised with HR directly.

---

## 4. Salary Reviews and Merit Increases

- Base salary is formally reviewed once per year, tied to the annual performance review cycle (see the Performance Management and Reviews Policy, POL-HR-011). Merit increases, if awarded, take effect **January 1** of the following year.
- Merit increase budgets are set annually by Finance based on company performance and market data, and are not guaranteed in any given year.
- Promotions that occur mid-year (see POL-HR-011 §5) include a compensation adjustment effective on the promotion's effective date, independent of the annual merit cycle.
- Employees may request an off-cycle compensation review if they believe their pay has fallen significantly behind market rate; such requests go through the employee's manager to HR and are evaluated using current market benchmarking data, not guaranteed to result in a change.

---

## 5. Bonuses and Incentive Pay

- **Annual bonus:** Most full-time employees are eligible for an annual discretionary bonus, targeted as a percentage of base salary that varies by role level (typically 5–20%). Bonus payout depends on both company performance and individual performance rating.
- **Sales incentive compensation:** Sales roles are covered by a separate commission plan documented in their offer letter and the annual Sales Compensation Plan, not by the discretionary bonus described above.
- **Spot bonuses:** Managers may award smaller, one-time spot bonuses (up to $500 without additional approval, up to $2,000 with department-head approval) to recognize exceptional work outside the normal cycle.
- Bonuses are paid in the **March** payroll cycle following the performance year they relate to, contingent on the employee being actively employed on the payout date.

---

## 6. Pay Equity

- Acme Corp conducts an annual **pay equity audit**, reviewing compensation across gender, race, and other protected characteristics for employees in comparable roles, and takes corrective action where unexplained gaps are identified.
- Acme Corp does not ask candidates for salary history during the hiring process, consistent with pay-equity best practice and applicable state law.
- Employees have the right to discuss their own pay with colleagues; Acme Corp will never discipline or retaliate against an employee for doing so, consistent with the National Labor Relations Act.

---

## 7. Direct Deposit and Pay Stubs

- Direct deposit information is managed in Workday under "Pay" → "Payment Elections." Changes must be submitted at least **5 business days** before a payday to take effect for that cycle.
- Digital pay stubs are available in Workday immediately after each pay run and include a full breakdown of gross pay, deductions, taxes, and net pay.
- Lost or incorrect direct deposit issues should be reported to **payroll@acmecorp.example.com** immediately; Acme Corp will issue a manual correction within 2 business days of a confirmed payroll error.

---

## 8. Garnishments and Deductions

- Acme Corp complies with all legally mandated wage garnishments (child support, tax levies, court-ordered judgments) and will notify the affected employee in writing when a garnishment order is received.
- Standard payroll deductions include federal/state/local taxes, benefits premiums (see the Employee Benefits Policy, POL-HR-004), and any voluntary deductions the employee has elected (401(k), HSA/FSA contributions, parking, etc.).

---

## 9. Equity Compensation

- Select roles (typically Senior level and above, or as approved during offer negotiation) receive **Restricted Stock Units (RSUs)** as part of total compensation, granted under Acme Corp's Equity Incentive Plan.
- Standard vesting schedule: **4 years, with a 1-year cliff** — 25% vests on the first anniversary of the grant date, with the remainder vesting monthly thereafter. No vesting occurs before the 1-year cliff.
- Refresh grants are considered annually as part of the merit and promotion cycle described in the Performance Management and Reviews Policy (POL-HR-011 §3–§5), at the discretion of the employee's department head and Compensation team.
- Vested RSUs are settled in shares (for public-company equivalents) or tracked as phantom equity value where applicable, net of applicable tax withholding, consistent with the employee's elected tax withholding method in Workday.
- Equity grants are detailed in the employee's individual grant agreement, which governs in the event of any conflict with this summary; questions should be directed to **compensation@acmecorp.example.com**.
- Unvested equity is forfeited upon voluntary resignation or termination for cause; vesting treatment upon a layoff or other involuntary separation not for cause is determined by the specific grant agreement and reviewed by Compensation on a case-by-case basis in coordination with the Termination and Offboarding Policy (POL-HR-013).
