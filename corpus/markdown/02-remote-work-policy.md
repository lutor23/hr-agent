# Remote Work Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-002  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Human Resources & Legal  

---

## 1. Overview

Acme Corp supports flexible work arrangements, including remote work, where operationally feasible. This policy governs eligibility, expectations, and compliance requirements for employees working remotely, whether from home, a co-working space, or temporarily from another state or country.

---

## 2. Eligibility

Remote work eligibility is determined by three factors:

1. **Role suitability:** Not all roles are eligible. Roles requiring physical presence (lab, manufacturing, on-site client support) are not eligible. Employees should confirm eligibility with their manager and HR.
2. **Performance standing:** Employees must be in good standing (no active Performance Improvement Plan) to maintain remote work privileges.
3. **Manager approval:** All remote work arrangements require explicit written approval from the employee's direct manager.

---

## 3. Types of Remote Work

### 3.1 Regular Remote Work (Hybrid or Fully Remote)
- Defined work location is the employee's home address on file in Workday.
- Arrangement must be documented in a Remote Work Agreement signed by employee and manager.
- Changes to the primary work location must be approved by HR.

### 3.2 Temporary Remote Work (Travel / Short-Term)
- Employees may work remotely from a different location for up to **10 consecutive business days** without additional approval beyond their manager's verbal/written consent.
- Longer temporary arrangements require formal approval (see Section 5).

---

## 4. In-State Remote Work

Employees working remotely within their state of employment have no additional tax or legal compliance obligations beyond their standard employment terms. Standard remote work approval applies.

---

## 5. Out-of-State Remote Work

Working from a different U.S. state for any period creates potential **tax nexus** and may create employment law obligations for Acme Corp in that state.

### 5.1 Short-Term (up to 10 business days per calendar year, cumulative)
- Manager approval required.
- Employee must notify HR via email at **hr@acmecorp.example.com** before travel.
- HR will confirm whether the state requires any additional action.

### 5.2 Extended Out-of-State (11–30 business days per calendar year)
- Manager approval required.
- **HR and Legal must approve** before the employee begins working from the new state.
- Legal will assess tax nexus, employment law applicability, and benefits implications.
- HR will issue a written determination within 5 business days of the request.

### 5.3 Permanent Relocation to Another State
- Treated as a change of employment jurisdiction.
- Requires a new offer letter or employment amendment.
- Compensation may be adjusted to reflect cost-of-labor differences.
- Full HR and Legal review required; timeline is typically 2–4 weeks.

---

## 6. International Remote Work

Working from outside the United States — including from a U.S. territory — triggers additional legal, tax, immigration, and data compliance obligations.

### 6.1 Eligibility
- **Not permitted** without prior written approval from HR, Legal, and the employee's VP-level or above.
- Approval is granted on a **case-by-case basis** and is not guaranteed.

### 6.2 Maximum Duration
- Approved international remote work is capped at **30 consecutive calendar days** per trip, and **60 calendar days per rolling 12 months**, unless a longer arrangement is approved through a formal international assignment process.

### 6.3 Required Approvals
1. Direct manager written approval.
2. HR review (immigration and tax exposure assessment).
3. Legal sign-off (data residency, employment law).
4. VP or above authorization.

### 6.4 Countries with Heightened Restrictions
Certain countries have additional restrictions due to data sovereignty laws, sanctions, or regulatory concerns. Employees must consult with Legal before working from any country listed in the **Restricted Countries Appendix** (maintained by Legal; contact legal@acmecorp.example.com).

---

## 7. Data Security While Remote

Regardless of location, employees must comply with the **Data Security Policy (POL-IT-001)**:
- Use only Acme-approved devices (see Equipment Policy POL-HR-008).
- Connect through the corporate VPN at all times when accessing company systems.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi without VPN enabled.
- Do not allow household members or third parties to access your work device or screen.
- Report any security incidents to **security@acmecorp.example.com** immediately.

---

## 8. Home Office Requirements

Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe, productive workspace. Acme Corp is not liable for injuries in a personal home workspace that fall outside workers' compensation coverage. Employees must:
- Have a dedicated workspace with a door or reasonable separation.
- Have reliable internet (minimum 25 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload recommended).
- Comply with all local zoning and lease/HOA rules regarding working from home.

---

## 9. Expense Reimbursement for Remote Work

See the **Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-HR-003)** for home office equipment and internet subsidy details. Key points:
- One-time home office setup stipend: up to $500 for approved full-time remote employees.
- Monthly internet subsidy: up to $50/month for employees whose primary work location is home.
- Standard home utilities (electricity, rent) are **not reimbursable**.

---

## 10. Revocation of Remote Work Privileges

Acme Corp reserves the right to revoke remote work arrangements at any time with **2 weeks' notice** for performance, security, or business reasons. Employees who cannot return to an in-office arrangement may be offered a transfer or separation with appropriate notice per their employment terms.
