# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy
**Document ID:** POL-HR-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Reviewed:** September 1, 2025  
**Owner:** Human Resources  

---

## 1. Overview

Acme Corp provides all eligible employees with Paid Time Off (PTO) to support personal well-being, work-life balance, and time away from work. PTO covers vacation, personal days, and routine illness. This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees in the United States.

---

## 2. Eligibility

- **Full-time employees** (30+ hours/week): Eligible from first day of employment.
- **Part-time employees** (20–29 hours/week): Eligible on a pro-rated basis.
- **Contractors and interns**: Not eligible for PTO under this policy.

---

## 3. PTO Accrual Rates

PTO accrues each pay period based on years of service:

| Years of Service | Annual PTO Days | Accrual per Pay Period (biweekly) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 year | 10 days (80 hrs) | 3.08 hrs |
| 1–3 years | 15 days (120 hrs) | 4.62 hrs |
| 3–5 years | 18 days (144 hrs) | 5.54 hrs |
| 5+ years | 20 days (160 hrs) | 6.15 hrs |

PTO begins accruing from the first day of employment and is available for use after a **30-day waiting period**.

---

## 4. PTO Request Process

### 4.1 Standard Requests (3+ business days in advance)
1. Employee submits request in the HR portal (Workday).
2. System notifies the employee's direct manager.
3. Manager approves or denies within 2 business days.
4. Employee receives confirmation via email.

### 4.2 Short-Notice Requests (less than 3 business days)
- Employee must contact their manager directly by phone or Slack before submitting the portal request.
- Manager has discretion to approve or deny based on business need.
- Denial must include a brief reason (e.g., "critical project deadline," "team coverage gap").

### 4.3 Extended Leave (10+ consecutive business days)
- Requires approval from both direct manager and the department head.
- HR must be notified at least 15 business days in advance when possible.
- A coverage plan must be submitted and approved before leave begins.

---

## 5. PTO Balances and Carryover

- **Maximum carryover:** Employees may carry over up to **40 hours (5 days)** of unused PTO into the next calendar year.
- **Payout cap:** Unused PTO beyond the 40-hour carryover cap is **forfeited**, not paid out, unless the employee is in California, where state law governs accrued PTO as wages.
- **Balance visibility:** Employees can view current PTO balance at any time in Workday under "Time Off."

---

## 6. PTO During Notice Period

PTO may not be used during a resignation notice period unless approved by HR and the department head in advance. Use of PTO during the notice period is generally discouraged.

---

## 7. Manager Responsibilities

- Ensure adequate team coverage before approving PTO.
- Respond to all PTO requests within 2 business days.
- Document all denials with a business justification in Workday.
- Never discourage employees from using PTO.

---

## 8. Interactions with Other Leave Types

| Leave Type | Interacts with PTO? |
|---|---|
| FMLA / medical leave | PTO may run concurrently with unpaid FMLA leave |
| Parental leave | Separate policy (POL-HR-005); PTO not required to be used first |
| Jury duty | PTO not required; separate jury duty pay applies |
| Bereavement | Separate policy (POL-HR-006); PTO may supplement |
| Holidays | Company holidays are separate; do not reduce PTO balance |
