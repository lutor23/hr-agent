Company Holidays Policy

Document ID: POL-HR-009

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Last Reviewed: September 1, 2025

Owner: Human Resources

1. Overview

Acme Corp provides paid holidays to all eligible employees. Company holidays are separate from PTO and do not reduce an employee's PTO balance. When a holiday falls on a weekend, the observed day is noted in the annual holiday schedule published each November.

2. Eligibility

  • Full-time employees (30+ hours/week): Eligible for all company holidays at full pay.
  • Part-time employees (20–29 hours/week): Eligible on a pro-rated basis proportional to scheduled hours.
  • Contractors and interns: Not eligible unless explicitly stated in their contract.

4. Floating Holidays

In addition to the schedule above, each full-time employee receives 2 floating holidays per calendar year. Floating holidays may be used for any purpose (religious observance, personal celebration, cultural holiday, etc.).

  • Floating holidays must be requested via Workday like a standard PTO request.
  • Floating holidays do not carry over to the next calendar year.
  • Unused floating holidays at year-end are forfeited.

5. Working on a Company Holiday

Non-exempt (hourly) employees required to work on a company holiday will receive:

  • Their regular pay for hours worked, plus
  • An additional day of PTO to be used within 90 days.

Exempt (salaried) employees who work on a holiday do not receive premium pay; they may take a substitute day off within 30 days with manager approval.

6. Religious and Cultural Observances

Acme Corp is committed to reasonable accommodation of sincerely held religious beliefs. Employees who need time off for religious observances not covered by the holiday schedule should:

  1. Notify their manager as early as possible (at least 2 weeks' notice preferred).
  2. Use a floating holiday, PTO, or request an unpaid day with manager approval.
  3. If no leave balance is available, contact HR at hr@acmecorp.example.com to discuss accommodation options.
